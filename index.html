About JLFLC

Where faith meets early learning

Jesus' Little Friends Learning Center, Inc. is a Christ-centred early childhood education centre serving families in Victorias City and Manapla, Negros Occidental.

We believe the earliest years lay the groundwork for everything that follows. Our job is to make those years safe, warm, and full of wonder — so that every little one leaves our gates confident, kind, and ready to learn.

Our Mission

To nurture every child holistically — spiritually, socially, emotionally and academically — within a safe, joyful and Christ-centred home.

Our Vision

To be a trusted second home where each learner grows in wisdom, in faith, and in the joy of discovering what they can do.

Learning Levels

A pathway that grows with them

From first steps to kindergarten readiness, each level is a gentle, guided step forward.

1

Toddler

Approx. 1–3 years

Gentle introduction to group life through sensory play, music, movement and lots of cuddles.

  • Sensory & motor play
  • Language through songs
  • Safe separation support
2

Nursery

Approx. 3–4 years

The wonder years: curiosity takes the lead as children begin letters, numbers and stories.

  • Pre-reading & pre-writing
  • Hands-on maths
  • Social skills & sharing
3

Kinder 1

Approx. 4–5 years

Structured discovery — blending, phonics and creative projects with plenty of delight.

  • Phonics & emergent reading
  • Art & music
  • Kinder readiness
4

Kinder 2

Approx. 5–6 years

Big-league readiness: confidence, concentration and a real love of learning.

  • Reading & writing readiness
  • Simple maths mastery
  • Independence & resilience

Age bands are indicative. Message us on Facebook and we'll help you find the right level for your child.

Life at JLFLC

A day in the life of a Little Friend

A gentle, predictable rhythm that keeps little ones secure — and busy in the best way.

Real photos from our classroom, below

  1. 7:00

    Warm welcome & free play

    Children are greeted by name, and the day begins with gentle, chosen play.

  2. 8:00

    Circle time & Bible story

    Songs, movement, a short Bible story and a simple lesson for the heart.

  3. 9:00

    Learning centres

    Small-group exploration of letters, numbers, shapes and the world around them.

  4. 10:00

    Snack & outdoor play

    A nutritious snack, fresh air, running around and friendships forming naturally.

  5. 11:00

    Stories & creative arts

    Read-alouds, painting, dancing and messy, wonderful hands-on fun.

  6. 1:00

    Rest, then home

    Quiet rest for younger ones, then a joyful send-off to waiting parents.
